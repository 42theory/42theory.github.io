Privacy

Last updated 19 November 2026

This site has no accounts, sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics or advertising. The lab and the film run entirely on your device. We receive personal information only when you write to us.

When you visit

42theory.com is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of each visitor for security purposes, whether or not the visitor is signed in to GitHub. We cannot see those logs. GitHub describes this under Data collection in its documentation.

Every file the pages need, including the fonts, comes from 42theory.com. The site contacts no other company while you read it.

The lab and the film

The neural code lab and the film are computed in your browser from a simulated population of neurons. Nothing you do in them leaves your device, and nothing in them is measured from you.

When you write to us

The contact form asks for your name, your email address, your organization if you choose to give it, and your message. When you press Send, your browser sends them to FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form delivery service, which passes them to our mailbox by email. FormSubmit's privacy policy says how it treats what passes through it. Our mailbox is hosted by IONOS.

If you would rather not use the form, write to hi@42theory.com directly. FormSubmit is then not involved.

We use what you send to answer you and to follow up on your request. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

How long we keep it

We keep correspondence for as long as it is needed to deal with your request and to keep a record of what was agreed. Ask us to delete your messages and we will.

Your choices

Write to hi@42theory.com to ask what we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Depending on where you live, the law may give you further rights, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If the way this site handles information changes, this page will say so, with a new date at the top.

Contact

42theory is responsible for this site. Questions about privacy go to hi@42theory.com.
