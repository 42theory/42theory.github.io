How this page works

Your picture is cropped to its centre square and read at 128 by 128 pixels, as in the study. Every frame on the stage is computed from it, in your browser: the cells, SLIC's four rounds, the borders and their integrals, the signed pass, and the comparison with squares.

Nothing leaves your device. The page's security policy lets it connect to no server, and the work runs in a worker on your machine.

Limits

  • The Study chapter's figures come from the study, one dataset at one picture size. This page does not run the trained model.
  • Colour kept measures the tokens, not a model's accuracy.
