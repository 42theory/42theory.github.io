In the study: accuracy and cost

The playground does not run the trained model. These figures are from the study: Imagewoof, a hard ten-class subset of ImageNet, at 128 pixels, 40 epochs, three seeds per variant, trained on two CPU threads.

  • Most of the gain comes from the regions. The signed borders' own lead, 0.2 points, is smaller than the spread between seeds.
  • The multiply-adds leave out building the regions. On two CPU threads that work dominates, and Perimeter runs at about half the speed of plain patches. Speed on a GPU is measured in the next study.

Cost against picture size

Patch tokens grow with the square of the picture's side. Perimeter's regions do not: its 36 seeds set the budget, not the pixels. This is Proposition 2, measured on your picture from 64 to 1,024 pixels.

Finding the regions takes longer as the picture grows, because the cells, the seeds' search and the borders cover every pixel. In the study this is why Perimeter runs at about half the speed of patches on a CPU.

How this page works

  1. The picture is cropped to its centre square and resized to 128 by 128 pixels, as in the study.
  2. Its pixels are averaged into a grid of 32 by 32 cells, four pixels on a side, each with a colour in CIE Lab.
  3. SLIC groups the cells into regions by colour and position: 36 seeds on a 6 by 6 lattice, four rounds, colour weight 10. The frame of the picture counts as one more cell around them.
  4. Every pair of regions that touch shares one border, directed from the lower number to the higher. Each border carries six Green integrals: area, two first moments and three second moments.
  5. One signed pass, the incidence matrix times the border integrals, gives every region its area, centre and spread. The page checks them against counting cells one by one.

The study's model also runs a learned stem of three convolutions and six transformer layers on these tokens. The playground does not: it has the tokenizer and the signed pass, and the accuracy figures above are the study's.

Nothing you give the playground leaves your device. The picture is read in your browser and measured in a worker on your machine, and this page's security policy does not let it connect to any server.

Keep the results

Limits

  • The study is one dataset at one picture size, trained for 40 epochs on two CPU threads. Small models on small datasets reward built-in structure, so the gaps may shrink or change sign at scale.
  • The regions are fixed before training: SLIC has no learned parameters, so a region can hold parts of two objects.
  • Colour kept is a property of the tokens. A model can do well or badly with either kind; the study measures that.
